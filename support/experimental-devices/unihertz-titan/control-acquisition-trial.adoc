= Offline control-region acquisition trial

Status on 2026-10-15: proposal only. Nothing in it has run on the phone, and
it authorizes no phone action. Each part needs the owner's explicit approval
of this procedure, and that approval covers one run.

The trial reuses the link:recovery-route-trial.adoc[recovery-partition route]:
write a pinned image to recovery with `fastboot flash`, start it with Volume
Up, let it restart into bootloader fastboot, and restore the original recovery
there. The image is the link:control/README.adoc[offline control-region
acquisition initramfs]. It copies the sixteen regions other than `userdata` to
the Mac while nothing else runs on the phone. That is the offline copy that
link:consistent-preservation.adoc[the preservation requirements] place before
any rollback write.

== Questions

. Does the control image start from recovery with Volume Up and bind its USB
  gadget, as the probe did?
. Does the transfer complete, with every region matching its first-pass hash
  on the Mac, and at what rate?
. Do both passes agree for every region, and are the four ext4 regions clean
  while Ubuntu Touch is not running?
. Does the image restart into fastboot by itself after the transfer, and does
  the restore work as before?

== Basis

* *Image.* The installed boot kernel and DTB, the control ramdisk (3773960
  bytes, `14a573ef...`), the probe's command line and the stock recovery's
  recovery-DTBO section, in the recovery layout: 13475840 bytes, SHA-256
  `41fd9c6bfb7f34f2351678eb52337ac6320672739d3109b0e8e0334b89d87b65`,
  byte-identical to the pinned `mkbootimg`. Padded with zeros to the
  partition's 33554432 bytes it has SHA-256
  `6729b1463f208f0ac73d3b950e3f8c3599f3343f99ce0addad4d5272ba421155`. It
  differs from the stock recovery in the same ways as the probe image, which
  the bootloader started.
* *What runs.* The probe's PID 1 path, which ran on the phone: storage guard,
  gadget bind and restart into the bootloader. Then two programs that have run
  only in VMs: the fixed region producer and the TTR1 sender. The producer
  reads the sixteen regions twice through exclusive read-only claims, with the
  kernel's volatile read-only flag set on every block device as in the probe
  trial. Nothing is written to the phone's storage and nothing is mounted from
  it.
* *Restart.* About five seconds after the Mac acknowledges the transfer,
  within about 35 seconds of a failure on the phone's side, and at the latest
  1800 seconds after PID 1 starts (a backstop follows ten seconds later). The
  producer stops after 1700 seconds and the sender after 1740, so a run without
  a listening Mac ends in about 29 minutes.
* *Duration.* The USB rate is unmeasured. At 1 MB/s the 771719168 bytes take
  about 13 minutes; below about 0.45 MB/s the run cannot finish and the phone
  restarts.
* *Mac side.* `tools/receive-control-regions.py` waits for the gadget's fixed
  strings, receives with the unchanged TTR1 receiver, and splits and checks
  the regions; see the link:control/README.adoc[image record]. It needs about
  1.6 GB of free space in the private evidence directory.
* *Flash helper.* `tools/flash-recovery.py` gained the `control-acquisition`
  profile; its identity checks and its single `fastboot flash recovery` are
  unchanged from the recovery-route trial, where both worked.

== Preconditions

* The recovery-route trial's preconditions: the owner present, a direct cable
  to the Mac, no system-image update pending or in progress and no command
  file in `/android/cache/recovery` or `/cache/recovery`, as Ubuntu Touch sees
  them. Battery at least 80%, because the run may take up to 30 minutes and
  charging in this environment is untested.
* The two private copies of the original recovery image (`4dd1c1f7...`), the
  second made for the recovery-route trial, both verified by hash before step
  5.
* A normal-mode control-region copy taken right before the trial, with `para`
  matching the latest copies (it still holds `bootonce-bootloader`), as in the
  recovery-route trial.
* At least 2 GB free on the Mac volume holding the private evidence directory.
* From step 5 until step 13 passes: no system update, factory reset,
  `reboot recovery` or Volume Up start other than step 9 and the rows below
  that name one.

== Procedure

*Part 1: write the control image.*

. *Baseline, normal mode, read-only*, as in the recovery-route trial, plus the
  update check above.
. *Check, owner-run:* `tools/acquire-partitions.py --sudo` into a new private
  directory, and the control-region copy. Recovery must be `4dd1c1f7...`, with
  boot (`6829a8bc...`), dtbo (`5b4e9f20...`) and `para` unchanged; otherwise
  stop.
. *Entry, owner-run:* `adb -d shell`, then `sudo /sbin/reboot bootloader`.
. *Recognition, read-only:* `tools/observe-fastboot.py observe`.
. *Write:*
+
[source,sh]
----
python3 support/experimental-devices/unihertz-titan/tools/flash-recovery.py \
  --profile control-acquisition \
  --image /absolute/private/usb-control-envelope-v2-recovery/UNTESTED-UNSIGNED-rescue.img \
  --output /absolute/private/new-recovery-flash
----
. *Return:* `tools/observe-fastboot.py return`; the owner unlocks the screen.
. *Check, owner-run:* `acquire-partitions.py` again. Recovery must be
  `6729b146...`, with boot and dtbo unchanged.

*Part 2: acquire.*

[start=8]
. *Receive:* start
  `tools/receive-control-regions.py --output /absolute/private/new-control-offline`.
  It waits up to 600 seconds for the image's USB device.
. *Start, owner-run:* keep the USB cable connected. Power the phone off from
  Ubuntu Touch. Hold Volume Up and press Power; release both once the
  unlocked-state warning appears.
. *Expected:* within about 30 seconds the receiver finds the device and the
  transfer begins. When it finishes, the receiver prints a summary of the
  sixteen regions, and about five seconds later the phone restarts into
  fastboot. Then run `observe-fastboot.py observe` with a 600-second wait to
  confirm fastboot. The screen may stay dark throughout.

*Part 3: restore.*

[start=11]
. *Write:* `flash-recovery.py --profile original-recovery --image
  /absolute/private/installed/recovery.img --output /absolute/private/new-recovery-restore`.
. *Return:* `observe-fastboot.py return`; the owner unlocks the screen.
. *Check, owner-run:* `acquire-partitions.py` again. Recovery must be
  `4dd1c1f7...`, with boot and dtbo unchanged.

*Part 4: compare, Mac only.* Compare each offline region with the latest
normal-mode copies and record which regions differ. This needs no phone.

== Stop conditions

The recovery-route trial's stop conditions apply, with its precedence rule:
whenever the phone is in bootloader fastboot while recovery holds the control
image or a partial image, run Part 3 there. Part 3 may be retried once. The
changes for this image:

[cols="2,3",options="header"]
|===
|Observation |Action
|Step 2 does not match
|Stop before writing anything.
|The flash helper refuses before sending
|Nothing was written. Return with `observe-fastboot.py return`.
|`fastboot flash` in step 5 fails, is refused, times out or crashes the bootloader
|Return to Ubuntu Touch, or let the crash fallback return it, and read recovery
first. If it is still `4dd1c1f7...`, stop: nothing was written. Otherwise go
to Part 3.
|Step 7 does not match
|Go to Part 3.
|Ubuntu Touch starts instead of the image in step 9
|One retry of step 9 is allowed if the boot reason is not `lk_crash`. Then
enter fastboot from Ubuntu Touch and go to Part 3.
|The receiver finds no device within 600 seconds
|Wait: without a listening Mac the image restarts into fastboot by itself
about 29 minutes after it starts. Then run `observe-fastboot.py observe` and go
to Part 3.
|The transfer stops or the receiver reports an error
|Keep the receiver's private output. If the phone's side failed, it restarts
into fastboot within about 35 seconds. If only the Mac's side failed, the phone
may wait for its time limits, about 29 minutes after it started; wait, or use
the physical fallback below and continue from Ubuntu Touch. Then go to Part 3.
Before Part 3, Part 2 may be repeated once if the owner approves the repeat
then: from fastboot, `observe-fastboot.py return`, then steps 8 to 10 again.
This overrides the precedence rule for that one repeat.
|The summary shows a region that changed between passes or an ext4 region that
is not clean
|Not a failure: keep the copy and record it. Go to Part 3.
|Neither fastboot nor Ubuntu Touch appears within 35 minutes of step 9
|Physical fallback: hold Power about ten seconds to force a restart, without
Volume Up, as in the recovery-route trial. From Ubuntu Touch, enter fastboot
and go to Part 3.
|The phone starts the image again instead of fastboot
|It runs and restarts again; if it repeats, use the physical fallback without
Volume Up.
|Ubuntu Touch cannot be reached while the image is installed
|Use the image's own route: start it with Volume Up and wait for its restart
into fastboot, with or without the receiver; then run Part 3.
|Step 13 does not match, or Part 3 fails twice
|Stop in the recovery-route trial's end state: avoid Volume Up starts,
`reboot recovery`, system updates and factory resets.
|Any step would write another partition, erase, or use `fastboot boot`
|Never do it; the helpers refuse.
|===

== Residual risks

* The producer and the sender have run only in VMs. On the vendor kernel an
  identity check, the exclusive claim or an ioctl may behave differently; the
  producer then refuses before streaming and the phone restarts.
* The phone's gadget enumerated on the Mac in the recovery-route trial, but no
  data has crossed it yet. A long transfer may stall or reset the link; the
  time limits end the run and the phone restarts.
* The run is long. Battery use, heat and charging during up to 30 minutes in
  this environment are untested.
* A vendor kernel crash during the run would restart through `panic=5` or the
  watchdog; so far such restarts have booted Ubuntu Touch, from which Part 3
  proceeds.
* The image's success path restarts from PID 1 instead of from the deadline
  process; both use the same restart call, which reached fastboot in the
  recovery-route trial.

== What this can and cannot show

Success gives a copy of the sixteen regions in which each region was read
twice through exclusive read-only claims, with the kernel read-only flag set
and no Ubuntu Touch, Android or modem software running, its identity checked
before and after, its ext4 state recorded where it has one, and its transfer
verified on the Mac before the acknowledgement. That meets the conditions of a
consistent copy except retention, since the Mac holds the only copy. It does
not rule out firmware or kernel writers outside the run, restore any region or
boot NixOS. `userdata` is excluded by the owner's decision.
